# src/lib
- Homepage trending ordering lives in home-ropes.ts after totals are joined, never in loadMatchups or the featured pool. Why: presentation ranking must not change shared read contracts or paid placement.
- Trend scouting uses read-only xAI Responses behind config(), a daily database lease, reserved three-rope budget and atomic capped draft pairs; both owner entry points share createModeratedOwnerMatchup. Why: no posting, moderation bypass, queue flood or repeated billed inference.
- Side emoji render as pinned Noto Color Emoji on both site and share cards through src/lib/emoji-art.ts (SVG on site, PNG in cards, same code; flags use Noto waved SVG on site and the Noto-rendered emoji-datasource-google PNG in cards because the Noto repo ships no PNG flags), falling back to the plain character; ordinary text emoji stay native; changing artwork bumps CARD_RENDER_VERSION and PULL_CARD_DESIGN. Why: one look per side on every device and no year-cached card with old artwork.
- Email dates and times go through formatUtcDateTime (src/lib/utc-format.ts): readable, always UTC-labelled, never localised; tests reject raw ISO strings in notice text. Why: recipients are worldwide and raw timestamps once reached a customer receipt.
- Owner-created matchups use source "owner", pass the same binding moderation, duplicate and slug rules as the AI generator, and create no order; creation revenue always excludes owner matchups. Why: house ropes are free and must never inflate income.
- The generator prompt, moderation rules, rules page and support answer are all built from the shared lists in content-policy.ts, including the real-person preference-or-merit rule. Why: what is published, generated and enforced must never drift apart.
- moderate() returns outcome allowed/rejected/unavailable; unavailable is never a verdict: callers keep content unpublished, never reject, refund or write a moderation_log verdict for it, and retry or ask again. Why: our outages must not cost customers what they bought.
- All email templates read colours via src/lib/email-templates/brand.tsx from src/lib/og/theme.ts. Why: one colour source.
