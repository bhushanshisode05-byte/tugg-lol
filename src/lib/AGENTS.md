# src/lib
- Side emoji render as pinned Noto Color Emoji on both site and share cards through src/lib/emoji-art.ts (SVG on site, PNG in cards, same code; flags use Noto waved SVG on site and the Noto-rendered emoji-datasource-google PNG in cards because the Noto repo ships no PNG flags), falling back to the plain character; ordinary text emoji stay native; changing artwork bumps CARD_RENDER_VERSION and PULL_CARD_DESIGN. Why: one look per side on every device and no year-cached card with old artwork.
- Email dates and times go through formatUtcDateTime (src/lib/utc-format.ts): readable, always UTC-labelled, never localised; tests reject raw ISO strings in notice text. Why: recipients are worldwide and raw timestamps once reached a customer receipt.
- Owner-created matchups use source "owner", pass the same binding moderation, duplicate and slug rules as the AI generator, and create no order; creation revenue always excludes owner matchups. Why: house ropes are free and must never inflate income.
- The generator prompt and moderation rules are built from one shared policy in content-policy.ts, including the real-person preference-or-merit rule. Why: the generator must never drift from what moderation allows.
